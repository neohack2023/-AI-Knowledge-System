# PR #67 Anti-Pattern Seed

Source PR: `#67` — review-convergence harness.  
Evidence state: confirmed/validated local negative knowledge.  
Disposition of source PR: stopped at breaker with a blocking finding; the lessons remain valid even though the candidate was not merge-eligible.

## `AP-R67-001` — Reusing evidence after candidate movement

- **Observed behavior:** mechanical/review/authorization state could be treated as current after the candidate head changed.
- **Why reasonable:** the branch name and concern were unchanged.
- **Actual failure:** trust-bearing evidence described older immutable bytes.
- **Repair:** bind each evidence class to exact candidate/artifact identity.
- **Guard:** candidate mismatch must stale evidence unless transfer is mechanically proven.
- **Promotion:** `PROMOTED_COMMON` → `PR-COMMON-001`.

## `AP-R67-002` — Resolving threads mistaken for current clean review

- **Observed behavior:** all known review threads could be resolved after repair and visually appear complete.
- **Actual failure:** the repair changed the candidate; no evidence proved the new head had no new finding.
- **Repair:** require current-head review at the required class.
- **Guard:** zero unresolved threads is never sufficient final-review evidence.
- **Promotion:** `PROMOTED_COMMON` → `PR-COMMON-003`.

## `AP-R67-003` — Green CI mistaken for merge authorization

- **Observed behavior:** exact-head CI could be green while review/owner authorization remained incomplete.
- **Actual failure:** deterministic checks were mistaken for mutation authority.
- **Repair:** preserve separate mechanical, review, eligibility, and owner gates.
- **Guard:** no automatic `CI_PASS → MERGE` transition.
- **Promotion:** `PROMOTED_COMMON` → `PR-COMMON-004`.

## `AP-R67-004` — Review identity confused with review class

- **Observed behavior:** a current scoped review could satisfy freshness even where a current FULL review was required.
- **Actual failure:** identity freshness and review depth were collapsed.
- **Repair:** represent reviewed head and review kind as separate obligations.
- **Guard:** STANDARD non-repair/SENSITIVE fixtures reject scoped-only current review when FULL is required.
- **Promotion:** `PROMOTED_COMMON` → `PR-COMMON-002`.

## `AP-R67-005` — Transition classification treated as timeless state

- **Observed behavior:** `repairOnlyDelta` / `scopeExpanded` could be carried across later heads.
- **Actual failure:** an H1→H2 classification was incorrectly reusable for H3.
- **Repair:** bind classification to exact base and head.
- **Guard:** stale/unbound classification fails closed to FULL review.
- **Promotion:** `PROMOTED_AREA` → `PR-HARNESS-001`.

## `AP-R67-006` — Risk classification not bound to candidate

- **Observed behavior:** a caller could theoretically carry `LOW` risk into a later candidate introducing sensitive scope.
- **Actual failure:** stale risk could lower the required review class.
- **Repair:** bind risk assessment to candidate identity or maintain monotonic effective risk across transitions.
- **Guard:** candidate movement with stale LOW cannot become merge-eligible without current risk evidence.
- **Promotion:** `PROMOTED_AREA` → `PR-HARNESS-002`.

## `AP-R67-007` — Later scoped review erases same-head FULL evidence

- **Observed behavior:** `latestReviewKind=SCOPED_REPAIR` could hide a FULL review already recorded for the same immutable head.
- **Actual failure:** evidence history was modeled as a single latest label instead of cumulative proof for one artifact.
- **Repair:** preserve current FULL evidence independently of latest narrower review kind.
- **Guard:** FULL@H followed by SCOPED@H still satisfies current FULL evidence for H.
- **Promotion:** `PROMOTED_AREA` → `PR-HARNESS-003`.

## `AP-R67-008` — Breaker exhaustion silently extended

- **Observed behavior:** a new valid blocking finding appeared after the configured SENSITIVE repair budget was consumed.
- **Actual failure avoided:** continuing automatically would recreate the unbounded repair hydra the feature was meant to stop.
- **Repair:** stop at `ADJUDICATE_STOP` and require a separately authorized continuation/new episode.
- **Guard:** breaker exhaustion + blocking finding cannot enter automatic repair or merge.
- **Promotion:** `PROMOTED_COMMON` → `PR-COMMON-006`.
